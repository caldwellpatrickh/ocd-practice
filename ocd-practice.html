Before you start, connect your private data storage.

Your incident logs are stored in your own private Google Sheet — no one else can see your data. This takes about 3 minutes to set up and only needs to be done once.

Open Apps Script in your new Sheet
In your copied Sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script. Delete any existing code and paste in the script provided below.

Daily practice
5-minute meditation
This should feel mildly uncomfortable — not soothing. You're training: thought ≠ threat.
5:00
Ready

Press start when you're ready. Find a comfortable seat. This is not to eliminate thoughts — it's to change your relationship to them.

What you're doing in each minute
Minute 1 Settle

Just sit. Notice your breath without changing it. Say internally: "This is not to eliminate thoughts. This is to change my relationship to them."

Minute 2 Thought labeling

When a thought appears — "that surface is contaminated," "my hands are dirty" — mentally say "OCD thought" or "contamination story." Nothing else. No arguing, no correcting, no reassuring. Label and return to breath.

Minute 3 Anxiety allowance

If anxiety rises, say internally "anxiety is allowed." Notice where it lives in your body — chest, stomach, throat. Observe it like weather passing through. Do not try to solve it or make it go away.

Minute 4 Urge observation

If the urge to wipe or use a glove shows up, say "there is the urge," rate it 0–10 silently, and do nothing else. Watch it rise, plateau, and move.

Minute 5 Reclaim authority

End with: "Thoughts are not commands. Urges are not emergencies. I choose behavior, not OCD."

Remember

Do not use this to lower anxiety before touching something. That's avoidance. Once daily, not during a panic spike.

Meditation is practice. ERP is treatment. Life is exposure.
